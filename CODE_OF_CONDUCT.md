# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Zap community a welcoming, respectful, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, language, or location.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith, provide useful technical context, welcome different viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Please protect private information and avoid sharing credentials, personal data, or proprietary material.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, hate speech, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, malicious disruption, impersonation, spam, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. Security vulnerabilities must be reported privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy. They may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban accounts for behavior that violates this Code of Conduct. Decisions will be made proportionately and with the safety of the community in mind.

To report a conduct concern, contact the project maintainers through the repository's private contact or security reporting channel. Please include links, dates, relevant context, and any supporting evidence. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies to project spaces, issue and pull request discussions, code review, documentation, release coordination, and official community events.
